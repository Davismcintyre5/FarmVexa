Built in Kenya, for Kenya.

HDM is a software company based in Eldoret, Kenya. We build practical tools for agriculture and business — designed around the phone in a farmer's hand and the realities of East African infrastructure.

Our flagship product, FarmVexa, is a registered copyright work (RZ104490) and runs on a live MERN stack with integrated M-Pesa payments, IoT sensors, and AI-driven crop diagnostics.

We believe the next decade of agricultural productivity in Africa will come from tools that start where farmers already are — not from systems that ask them to change everything.
