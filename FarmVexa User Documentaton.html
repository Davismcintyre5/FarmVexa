Getting Started

System Requirements

PlatformRequirements
Web AppChrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Edge 90+, Safari 14+
DesktopWindows 10+, macOS 12+, Ubuntu 20.04+
AndroidAndroid 8.0 or later

Accessing FarmVexa

Available via web browser at farmvexa.pxxl.click, desktop applications for Windows/macOS/Linux, and Android APK.

Registration

  1. Visit farmvexa.pxxl.click.
  2. Click Register or Get Access.
  3. Fill in your details: name, email, phone, password, county, sub-county.
  4. Agree to terms and submit.
  5. Wait for admin approval (within 24 hours).
  6. Receive welcome email with login instructions.

Note: If self-registration is disabled, use the Get Access page to request an account.

Creating Your First Farm

  1. Login to your account.
  2. Click Create Farm.
  3. Enter farm name, county, sub-county, and size.
  4. Your farm is ready.

Module Guides

1. Dashboard

Available for: All roles

The dashboard is the first screen after login. It shows:

  • Farms: Number of farms (farmers) or assigned farm (team).
  • Alerts: Unread high/critical alerts count.
  • Animals: Total active animals.
  • Stock Value: Total production stock value in KES.

Recent alerts show the latest 4 unread alerts. Quick actions provide shortcuts to Scan Crop, AI Chat, Production, and Alerts.

2. Farms & Fields

Available for: Farmer (CRUD), Team (view)

Farms have name, location (county/sub-county), and size. Fields within farms have crop type and soil type. Farm detail shows Fields, Animals, Stock Value, and Alerts stats.

3. IoT & Sensors

Available for: All roles

Scan Crop: Capture or upload crop leaf photos for AI disease detection.

Sensors: Real-time temperature, humidity, soil moisture, and light with trend indicators.

Devices: ESP32 registration with auto online/offline detection.

4. Livestock

Available for: Farmer/Manager (CRUD), Worker/Vet (view)

Individual animal registration with tag ID, type, breed, weight, and status. Poultry batch mode for bulk registration with mortality tracking.

5. Health

Available for: Farmer/Vet/Manager (CRUD), Worker (locked)

Record vaccinations, treatments, checkups, diseases, and deworming. Track upcoming and overdue vaccinations. System sends reminders at 7, 3, and 0 days before due date.

Warning: Workers see "accessible by farm management and veterinarians only."

6. Production

Available for: Farmer/Manager/Worker (record), Vet (view)

Dual-mode: Animal (milk, eggs, meat) and Crop (harvest). Field selection auto-fills crop type. Quantity auto-calculates value from pricing. Production auto-adds to stock.

7. Inventory & Stock

Available for: Farmer/Manager (CRUD), others (view)

Prod Stock (auto from production) and Farm Inventory (purchased items). Stock in/out with full movement history. Low stock alerts with minimum thresholds.

8. Finance

Available for: Farmer/Manager only

Sales cart with multiple items, discounts, and printable receipts. Expense recording by category. Product pricing with auto-suggestions from farm data. Income/Expense/Net summaries.

9. Weather

Available for: All roles

Current conditions, 7-day forecast, severe weather alerts (rain, heatwave, frost, drought, storm), and farming recommendations. Weather is auto-fetched from your farm's county/sub-county location.

10. Team & Tasks

Available for: Farmer (CRUD), Manager (tasks), Worker/Vet (view)

Team: Add workers, managers, and vets. Members receive login credentials via email.

Tasks: Create tasks, assign to team members, set priority (Low/Medium/High/Urgent), track status (Pending → In Progress → Completed).

11. Farmers Market

Available for: Farmer only (in dashboard), Public (browse + inquire)

Market Overview

The Farmers Market allows farmers to list their farm products for public viewing. Buyers browse products, view farm details, and contact farmers directly via SMS or email inquiry. The market can be enabled or disabled by the platform administrator.

When disabled, farmers see a notice with support contact. When enabled, products appear on the public market page and a preview section on the landing page.

Adding Products

  1. Go to Operations → Market.
  2. Click Add Product.
  3. Enter product name, category, price, unit, quantity.
  4. Upload up to 5 photos.
  5. Enter contact phone, WhatsApp, and email.
  6. Select farm (auto-populates county/sub-county).
  7. Add exact direction for buyers.
  8. Click Add Product.

Product Categories

  • 🥬 Vegetables
  • 🍎 Fruits
  • 🐄 Livestock
  • 🐔 Poultry
  • 🥛 Dairy
  • 🌾 Grains
  • 📦 Other

Product Status

  • Active: Visible to public buyers.
  • Sold: Marked after sale, hidden from public.
  • Inactive: Hidden from public, can reactivate.

Managing Inquiries

Buyers send inquiries from the public market page. You receive SMS and email with buyer details and message. Mark inquiries as read or delete them.

Public Market Experience

Buyers browse at farmvexa.pxxl.click/market. They can search products, filter by category, view photos, see location and contact details, and send inquiries without logging in.

12. Reports

Available for: Farmer/Manager

9 report types: General, Stock, Livestock, Production, Crops, Financial, Vaccination, Inventory, Tasks. Filter by period. Print-optimized formatting with farm header and summary.

13. AI Chat

Available for: All roles

Chat with FarmVexa AI in English or Kiswahili. The AI knows your farm — animals, production, stock, weather, alerts, and devices. Ask questions like:

  • "How is my farm today?"
  • "What is the current weather?"
  • "When is the next vaccination due?"
  • "How much milk did we produce this week?"

Daily usage limits apply. The AI shows farm-specific data in responses.

14. Alerts & Notifications

Available for: All roles

Automatic alerts for:

  • Low soil moisture
  • High temperature
  • Disease risk conditions
  • Disease detected in crops
  • Device offline
  • Low stock
  • Vaccination due
  • Equipment maintenance due
  • Weather warnings

High and critical alerts send email. Critical alerts can also send SMS. Header bell shows real-time unread count.

15. Settings

Available for: All roles

  • Profile: Update name, phone, county, sub-county.
  • Password: Change password with current password verification.
  • Support: View support contacts (phone, email, WhatsApp).
  • Downloads: Download desktop and mobile apps.

Mobile Access

FarmVexa is fully responsive and works on mobile browsers. Install as PWA:

  1. Open FarmVexa in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap menu (⋮) → Install App or Add to Home Screen.
  3. FarmVexa appears on your home screen.

The crop scanner uses your phone camera for instant disease detection. Android APK also available for download.

Desktop Applications

Download for Windows, macOS, or Linux from the Downloads page or Settings page in your dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FarmVexa?

An AI-powered farm intelligence platform combining IoT, computer vision, and AI for smarter farming.

How does the Farmers Market work?

Farmers list products with photos and prices. Buyers browse publicly and send inquiries. Farmers receive SMS/email and respond directly.

Can I sell on the market as a worker?

No. Only farmers (farm owners) can list products.

How many photos can I upload?

Up to 5 photos per product.

Is the market always open?

No. The platform administrator can enable or disable it.

Can my team members use it?

Yes! Add workers, managers, and vets with role-based permissions.

Does it work offline?

Web app requires internet. Desktop app caches some data.

What support is available?

Phone, email, and WhatsApp. Response within 24 hours.

How does crop scanning work?

Take a photo of a crop leaf. AI analyzes for diseases, pests, and deficiencies.

How do I connect sensors?

Connect ESP32-based IoT sensors. Register device in dashboard. Data flows automatically.
