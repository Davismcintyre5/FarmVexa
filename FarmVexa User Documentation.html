3. IoT & Sensors

Available for: All roles

Devices: ESP32 registration with zone (Field/Storage/Greenhouse/Livestock) and sensor type (DHT/CO2/PIR). Auto online/offline detection.

4. How Sensors Work

FarmVexa uses small electronic devices called sensors to monitor your farm automatically. These devices are placed in your field or storage room and send readings to your dashboard every minute without you doing anything.

Types of Sensors

SensorWhat It MeasuresWhere It GoesWhat It Detects
DHT22Temperature + HumidityField or StorageHeat, moisture, disease risk
Soil MoistureWater content in soilFieldDry soil, overwatering
Light SensorSunlight levelFieldShade, cloud cover
CO2 SensorCarbon dioxideStorageInsect activity (weevils, beetles)
PIR MotionMovementStorageRats and rodents

How to Connect a Sensor

  1. Purchase an ESP32 sensor device.
  2. Power it on and connect to WiFi.
  3. Go to Devices → Register Device.
  4. Enter the Device ID (unique name for your sensor).
  5. Select Zone: Field or Storage.
  6. Select Sensor Type: DHT, CO2, PIR, etc.
  7. Click Register Device.

Once registered, the sensor starts sending data automatically. You'll see readings under Sensors and receive alerts when conditions are unfavourable.

Example: A DHT sensor in your storage room sends temperature and humidity every minute. If temperature goes above 30°C, you get an alert saying "Storage temperature high" with advice to ventilate.

Where to View Sensor Data

  • Field tab: Temperature, humidity, soil moisture, light level
  • Storage tab: Temperature, humidity, CO2, motion (rats)
  • Device detail: Recent readings + battery level

5. Connecting HDM Stream

HDM Stream (stream.hdm.co.ke) is a camera streaming system that works with FarmVexa. It allows live video streaming, photo capture, video recording, and field scanning from your phone or drone camera.

What You Need

  • A phone or device with HDM Stream open
  • Internet connection (WiFi or mobile data)
  • Your FarmVexa account

HDM Stream Modes

ModePurpose
Send Mode (stream.hdm.co.ke/out)Camera device — streams video, captures photos, records video
Receive Mode (stream.hdm.co.ke/in)Viewer — watches live stream, captures photos

How HDM Stream Captures Photos

  1. Open HDM Stream on your camera device.
  2. Camera starts automatically.
  3. Tap 📸 Capture to take a photo.
  4. The photo uploads automatically with a watermark.
  5. Photo appears in Gallery.

How HDM Stream Records Video

  1. Open HDM Stream on your camera device.
  2. Tap ⏺ Record to start recording.
  3. The video records from the live camera.
  4. Tap ⏹ Stop Rec to stop.
  5. Video saves to Gallery for download.

How HDM Stream Streams Live Video

  1. Open HDM Stream Send Mode on the camera device.
  2. Open HDM Stream Receive Mode on the viewer device.
  3. Both devices must be logged in.
  4. On Send Mode, select the receiver and tap Send.
  5. Live video appears on the Receive Mode screen.
  6. Frames stream every 0.2 seconds (5 frames per second).

How HDM Stream Sends Photos to FarmVexa

When HDM Stream is opened inside FarmVexa (via External Camera button), captured photos can be sent directly to FarmVexa:

  1. Open FarmVexa → Scan Crop or Field Scan.
  2. Click Open External Camera.
  3. HDM Stream opens inside FarmVexa.
  4. Capture photos on HDM Stream.
  5. Tap 🌾 Send to FarmVexa on the photo.
  6. The photo appears in FarmVexa ready for AI analysis.
Note: The "Send to FarmVexa" button only appears when HDM Stream is opened from FarmVexa. If opened directly in a browser, it shows "Open from FarmVexa to send photos."

6. Field Scan

Available for: Farmer, Manager, Worker, Vet

Field Scan allows you to scan an entire field using the HDM Stream live camera. Photos are auto-captured at intervals with GPS coordinates and analyzed by AI for diseases, weeds, and pests.

How Field Scan Works

  1. Go to Field Scan from the sidebar.
  2. Select your farm and field.
  3. Select the crop type.
  4. Click Open External Camera.
  5. On HDM Stream, tap 🌾 Start Field Scan.
  6. Walk or fly the camera across the field.
  7. Photos capture automatically every few seconds with GPS.
  8. Tap ⏹ Stop when done.
  9. All photos auto-send to FarmVexa.
  10. AI analyzes the photos (under 5 minutes).
  11. Results show disease count, weed spots, and health percentage.

During the Scan You'll See

  • 📸 Photo counter (e.g., 26/80)
  • ⏱ Elapsed time
  • 📍 GPS coordinates with accuracy
  • 📡 Live stream indicator
  • Progress bar

Field Scan Results

  • Diseases: Count, severity, and GPS locations.
  • Weed Spots: Areas with weed pressure.
  • Healthy Percentage: Overall crop health.
  • Photos: All analyzed frames with AI results.
  • Recommendations: Treatment advice from AI.

Viewing Scan History

Click History on the Field Scan page. Each scan shows date, field, crop, photos analyzed, diseases detected, and health percentage. Click any scan for full details.

Note: Field Scan must be enabled by the administrator. If disabled, you'll see a message with support contacts.

7. Storage Monitoring

Available for: Farmer, Manager

Storage Monitoring keeps watch over your grain store or storage room. Sensors detect unfavourable conditions that attract pests or cause spoilage.

What Storage Monitoring Detects

SensorWhat It DetectsWarningCritical
DHT22 (Temperature)Heat that speeds up insect breedingAbove 30°CAbove 35°C
DHT22 (Humidity)Moisture that causes mouldAbove 65%Above 75%
CO2 (MH-Z19)Insect respiration (weevils, beetles)Above 800 ppmAbove 1200 ppm
PIR MotionRats and rodents at night—Motion detected

Setting Up Storage Monitoring

  1. Register a device with zone: Storage.
  2. Select sensor type: DHT, CO2, or PIR.
  3. Place the ESP32 with sensors in your storage room.
  4. Sensors send data every 60 seconds automatically.
  5. Alerts trigger when thresholds are crossed.

Alert Notifications

  • Warning alerts: Shown in your dashboard only.
  • Critical alerts: Email + SMS sent immediately.
  • Cooldown: Same alert not sent more than once per 6 hours.
Example: If temperature hits 36°C and humidity 78%, you receive "Storage temperature critical" and "Storage humidity critical" alerts with recommendations to ventilate and dehumidify.

Viewing Storage Data

Go to Sensors → Storage tab. Select your storage device. You'll see:

  • Current temperature and humidity
  • CO2 level (if CO2 sensor connected)
  • Motion status (if PIR sensor connected)
  • Reading history table

Prod Stock (auto from production) and Farm Inventory (purchased items). Stock in/out with full movement history. Low stock alerts with minimum thresholds.

15. Farmers Market

Available for: Farmer only (in dashboard), Public (browse + inquire)

Farmers list their farm products for public viewing. Buyers browse products, view farm details, and contact farmers directly via SMS or email inquiry. The market can be enabled or disabled by the platform administrator.

Adding Products

  1. Go to Operations → Market.
  2. Click Add Product.
  3. Enter product name, category, price, unit, quantity.
  4. Upload up to 5 photos.
  5. Enter contact phone, WhatsApp, and email.
  6. Select farm (auto-populates county/sub-county).
  7. Add exact direction for buyers.
  8. Click Add Product.

Product Categories

  • 🥬 Vegetables
  • 🍎 Fruits
  • 🐄 Livestock
  • 🐔 Poultry
  • 🥛 Dairy
  • 🌾 Grains
  • 📦 Other

Product Status

  • Active: Visible to public buyers.
  • Sold: Marked after sale, hidden from public.
  • Inactive: Hidden from public, can reactivate.

Managing Inquiries

Buyers send inquiries from the public market page. You receive SMS and email with buyer details and message. Mark inquiries as read or delete them.

Public Market Experience

Buyers browse at farmvexa.hdm.co.ke/market. They can search products, filter by category, view photos, see location and contact details, and send inquiries without logging in.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FarmVexa?

An AI-powered farm intelligence platform combining IoT, computer vision, and AI for smarter farming.

How does Field Scan work?

Open the external camera, start the field scan from HDM Stream, and photos are auto-captured with GPS. AI analyzes them for diseases, weeds, and pests.

What is Storage Monitoring?

Sensors in your grain store detect temperature, humidity, CO2 (insects), and motion (rats). Alerts notify you when conditions become unfavourable.

How does HDM Stream work?

HDM Stream streams live video from your camera device to your viewer. It captures photos and records video. When opened from FarmVexa, photos can be sent directly for AI analysis.

How do sensors send data?

Sensors connect to WiFi and send readings to your FarmVexa dashboard automatically every minute. No manual entry needed.

How does the Farmers Market work?

Farmers list products with photos and prices. Buyers browse publicly and send inquiries. Farmers receive SMS/email and respond directly.

Can I sell on the market as a worker?

No. Only farmers (farm owners) can list products.

How many photos can I upload?

Up to 5 photos per product.

Is the market always open?

No. The platform administrator can enable or disable it.

Can my team members use it?

Yes! Add workers, managers, and vets with role-based permissions.

Does it work offline?

Web app requires internet. Desktop app caches some data.

What support is available?

Phone, email, and WhatsApp. Response within 24 hours.

How does crop scanning work?

Take a photo of a crop leaf. AI analyzes for diseases, pests, and deficiencies.

How do I connect sensors?

Connect ESP32-based IoT sensors. Register device in dashboard with zone and sensor type. Data flows automatically.

What sensors detect storage pests?

CO2 sensors detect insect respiration (weevils/beetles). PIR motion sensors detect rats. DHT sensors detect conditions that attract pests.
