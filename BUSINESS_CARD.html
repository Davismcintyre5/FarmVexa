⚠️ The Problem

Farmers discover crop diseases, soil problems, storage pests, and livestock issues after damage has already occurred. Manual monitoring misses subtle changes. No historical data for decisions. Reactive farming leads to losses.

✅ How FarmVexa Solves It

See — Computer vision + live camera scans crops for diseases.
Sense — IoT sensors monitor soil, storage & environment 24/7.
Predict — AI analyzes trends and predicts pest/disease risks.
Grow — Data-driven decisions for better yields.
